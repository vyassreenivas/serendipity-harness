Reliability compares the same interviews coded in two rounds: agreement is the share of interviews where both codings agree on a code; kappa and AC1 correct it for chance (AC1 is not pulled to zero by codes found in nearly every interview). Part of the difference comes from the codebook itself changing between the two rounds.
