1. Parts of serendipity SQ1

Which parts are present?

Pick one for each story: What was the founder doing when it happened?

1.1Not looking at all

The founder was not looking for a business or an idea. A need and a way to meet it turned up together in everyday life, and they saw an opportunity.

Use it when
The founder was not looking for a business or a solution when it happened.
Example
'I made it for myself, then people kept asking for one.' (made up)
Don't use it when
The founder already wanted to start some business. Use "Open to any business". carried over from the version before
