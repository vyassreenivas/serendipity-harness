64. Interview 64

4 stories. A long-time executive in educational photography sees event organisers struggle while working with universities and decides to build an online ticketing platform, validates it with market research, builds it with an experienced team he calls lucky to have, and grows through a soft launch, ads and content. The COVID pandemic brought a rise in event-platform adoption and a major transformation, including built-in video conferencing that drew more clients. The account is mostly planned and research-driven; luck language appears in passing.

  • S1 the ticketing platform idea: Through his work in educational photography he saw the problems event organisers face and decided to build an online ticketing and booking system, then validated it with market research.
  • S2 building the first product: The team scopes, designs, tests and releases the first product, which he says was hard, and luckily he had experienced people helping.
  • S3 launch and growth: A soft launch with lower-risk clients, then advertising and content; slow at first, then over $1M in tickets in year one; he says they are fortunate and luckily met financial targets.
  • S4 the COVID transformation: The COVID pandemic brought a massive rise in adoption of event platforms; the platform transformed and added embedded virtual video conferencing, which encouraged more clients.
