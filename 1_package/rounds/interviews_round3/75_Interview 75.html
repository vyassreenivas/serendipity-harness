75. Interview 75

3 stories. Two freelancer friends from hard backgrounds deliberately set out to offer affordable tech training to unemployed Nigerian youths, built a site and physical hub, tested interest with a waitlist shared through social media, their church and friends, and drew more interest than the first cohort could take. A friend who liked what they were doing invested about $2K. The account is mostly planned and purposive; no event is called unexpected and no luck language appears.

  • S1 founding the tech training hub: Having grown up poor and worked as freelancers for foreign firms, the founder and his housemate co-founder teamed up to help youths facing unemployment move into tech, and built a website and furnished hub.
  • S2 the waitlist launch: A waitlist shared on social media, community groups, their pastor, church and friends grew slowly at first, then to 50 in a month and over 80 for 50 places.
  • S3 the friend's investment: After they put in about $2K themselves, a friend who loved what they were doing invested about $2K on agreed terms.
