52. Interview 52

4 stories. A golf and travel enthusiast who wanted a business built on his passions had the idea for a golf trip planning service while playing golf, launched it by partnering with resorts and telling people he knew, got his first client through a friend's referral, grew through a website and content, and was hit by pandemic travel restrictions before recovering. Little in the account is told as unexpected; the closest are the idea arriving during a game, business growth beyond expectation and the pandemic decline.

  • S1 the idea on the golf course: Wanting to make a living from golf and travel, and having rejected earlier business ideas, he had the idea for the golf trip business during a game and acted at once.
  • S2 the first client: A friend told a friend of his about the new business and put them in contact, which brought the first client.
  • S3 launch and online presence: He arranged resort partnerships, told people he knew, built a website with a friend's help, and grew through blog content, SEO and repeat customers after a slow start.
  • S4 the pandemic decline: Lockdowns and travel restrictions sharply cut activity for a year before business returned and grew.

Q: Take us through the process of designing your initial service/offering
[7]A1.3S21.3S41.6S22.8S43.4S25.5S46.7S47.1S2
A: The whole process starts with getting to know the clients and their needs. Each group is different and each poses a different challenge so I first have to figure out what would suit them best when it comes to a golf vacation. [9]A1.3S31.5S14.3S17.1S28.1S38.5S3
A: Before I made an official launch I had to first make up a list of some of the most popular golf resorts that I wanted to partner up with, reach out to them and sort out the various details of the partnership. After finishing up with this I had to come up with a list of possible clients and figure out the most fitting target audience. This wasn’t too hard for me as I am a golf enthusiast myself and have a great deal of knowledge in this area. I started by contacting the golf resorts I knew were of the highest quality then, after discussing a partnership with each of them, I started telling every golf enthusiast I knew about my new business in the hopes of attracting my first clients. Next, I had to make my newly founded business known among other golf enthusiasts and so I did.
