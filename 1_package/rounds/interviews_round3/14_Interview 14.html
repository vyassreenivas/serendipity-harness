A: My fascination with printing dates back to my childhood, when I was inherently curious about the mixing of four ink colors to create an array of hues, commonly referred to as CMYK. During my childhood, color printers were rare devices. I have been learning to edit code independently and experimenting with software development since high school. With a major in college related to software development, I have solidified my foundation in this field. In 2012, I took the first step in my career by founding a software development company that specializes in providing SaaS and ERP software development services to various manufacturing enterprises. The term 'print on demand' wasn't widely recognized in China then. The prevailing idea was that individuals could print their favorite artwork on T-shirts. Meanwhile, the demand for personalization was gaining prominence during this period. This trend made me realize that print-on-demand was an industry with significant potential. By 2016, the concept of print-on-demand slowly gained traction in China. Benefiting from my early experience in software development, I built a substantial network of contacts. Meanwhile, my background in IT allowed me to gradually establish partnerships with select factories, offering uncomplicated print-on-demand products and services. Initially, my foray into the field was limited to basic T-shirt printing. After a year of such collaboration, I established [COMPANY], marking my official entry into the print-on-demand industry.

Q: Describe the process of launching the business.
[9]A1.4S21.5S21.6S24.3S25.4S26.3S28.2S22.AS2
A: Shopify has gained immense popularity within China's cross-border e-commerce sector, with frequent mentions of the platform. Meanwhile, several print-on-demand company apps, such as Printful, Printify, and Teelaunch, were already present in the Shopify app store before our entry. We recognized the significant potential and traffic on the Shopify App Store, prompting us to submit [COMPANY] for review by Shopify app developers. To meet Shopify's standards, we diligently standardized our code by their developer documentation, ensuring user privacy protection and order status synchronization. Meanwhile, we embedded our app into the Shopify user backend, facilitating a seamless experience for customers within their stores, and rigorous compatibility testing under various browsers was conducted. This thorough process, taking approximately 45 days, resulted in the successful launching of [COMPANY] on the Shopify App Store. Given the novelty of our watch product and the relatively limited competition in the print-on-demand market at that time, Shopify recognized the value of our platform. As widely known, finding apps that cater to your needs in the Shopify App Store is a straightforward process—simply use keywords for your search. For instance, by using the keyword 'Print on Demand,' numerous print-on-demand apps will appear. Fortunately, within two months of [COMPANY]’s launch, Shopify not only approved our app but also recommended it and prominently featured it in the Shopify App Store. Within six months of launch, our app installations reached 1,000. In my opinion, the key to success in the Shopify app store lies in offering unique products and maintaining high product quality. Building on this milestone, we initiated a marketing campaign targeting e-commerce sellers. Through posts in various forums and Facebook groups frequented by e-commerce sellers, complemented by engaging videos, we showcased our products, directed customers to our product page, and highlighted [COMPANY]'s print-on-demand service. This strategic approach has led to consistent linear growth in the number of [COMPANY] users. According to preliminary statistics, implementing this strategy has resulted in a month-on-month increase of 30% in the number of users. As our business continues to grow, we've become acutely aware of the fundamental differences between print-on-demand and drop shipping. In the dropshipping model, there is no customization process, as it relies on existing products. However, with print-on-demand, a non-existent product is transformed into an existing one through the printing process. Acknowledging the unique requirements of print-on-demand, our factory production lines need to take proactive measures. This involves purchasing printing machines and materials suitable for creating the target products six months in advance.

Accurate Audience Targeting In our advertising strategy, Google Ads takes precedence as our primary paid marketing channel.
