# Stage 1: The research question

The first step of the harness, done by the researchers. It fixes what the
study asks before any paper is read for codes or any interview is coded.

## The files

| File | What it holds | Who writes it |
|---|---|---|
| `protocol.md` | The general instructions for any research question: three kinds of question, six steps from a research question to sub-questions and analysis groups, and the literature behind each step | Researchers |
| `research_question.md` | The protocol applied to serendipity, step by step (classification, parts, anchors, tests), then the central question, the unit of analysis (the episode), the six sub-questions, the eleven analysis groups, how they fit together, and the rules for misfits and calibration rounds | Researchers |
| `sources.md` | The past research behind the question and the groups, and what it suggests | Researchers, with a model's help in finding and summarising sources |

## Rules

1. **People decide.** A model may suggest wording or sources. The question,
   the sub-questions and the groups are the researchers' decisions.
2. **Agree before moving on.** Stage 2 starts only once `research_question.md`
   is marked AGREED and committed.
3. **Changes are commits.** Every later change is a new commit whose message
   says what changed and why. Nothing is rewritten silently.

## What Stage 2 takes from here

The sub-questions and groups are the frame for choosing papers (Stage 2):
every paper is chosen for the sub-questions it helps answer, with evidence.
