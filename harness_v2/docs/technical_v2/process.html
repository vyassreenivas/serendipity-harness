How the harness works

Every step of the harness, in order. Each is done by us, by the model or by a program, and each leaves a record. Outlined steps are where we decide. The same steps are drawn in Figures 3 and 4 of the technical draft.

UsModelProgram

UsResearch decisions: what to ask, what to read, what enters the codebook, what the findings mean.
ModelReading for meaning at a scale people cannot. Every output is checked by the program and decided by us.
ProgramWork that must come out the same every time: splitting, numbering, copying, checking, counting, applying rules. Free, instant, and anyone can rerun it.
